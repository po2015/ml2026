Scope of Work

Every element localized

E-learning localization is more than translating text. We adapt every layer of your course for the target learner.

  • UI text, menus, buttons, and navigation labels
  • Slide text, captions, and on-screen annotations
  • Audio narration — AI or human voice replacement
  • Embedded video with subtitles or full dubbing
  • Quiz questions, answer options, and feedback messages
  • Branching scenario dialogue and decision prompts
  • Interactive hotspots, drag-and-drop labels, and markers
  • Software simulations and screen recordings re-captured with localized UI
  • xAPI statements and verb definitions adapted per language
  • Certificate, completion page, and course thumbnail text
  • Accessibility: alt text, tab order, and WCAG contrast maintained
  • Culturally inappropriate imagery flagged and replaced
  • RTL layout adaptation (Arabic, Hebrew)
  • CJK typography and character spacing (Chinese, Japanese, Korean)

Process

From source course to global learners

1
Content Audit
We analyse your SCORM package or source files (.story, .cptx, XLIFF exports, or raw HTML5) to identify all translatable text, audio tracks, video elements, interactive layers, and images requiring cultural adaptation. You receive a fixed word count, media duration, and a per-element scope sheet before work begins.
2
Translation & Script Adaptation
Text is exported to XLIFF/Word for translation by subject-matter linguists, with your glossaries and translation memories applied for consistency across modules. Narration scripts are adapted and timed to match the original pacing so animations stay in sync, then re-imported into the authoring tool.
3
Multimedia Production
Audio narration is produced with AI or human voices and synced slide-by-slide to animations. Embedded video receives subtitles or full dubbing, on-screen graphics are rebuilt in the target language, and software simulations are re-captured with the localized interface.
4
Course Rebuild & Linguistic QA
The localized content is integrated back into the authoring tool and text overflow, truncated buttons, and misaligned triggers are fixed. A separate native-speaking QA reviewer then plays through the entire course, checking navigation, quiz logic, branching paths, hotspots, audio sync, and every visible string in context.
5
LMS Testing & Delivery
The published SCORM/xAPI package is tested in SCORM Cloud and, if required, in your specific LMS (Moodle, Cornerstone, SAP SuccessFactors, Workday, Docebo). We confirm completion tracking, pass/fail thresholds, resume behaviour, and certificate generation work correctly, then deliver the package alongside the localized source files for future updates.
