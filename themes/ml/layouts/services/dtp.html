Languages & Scripts

Typesetting for every writing system

Each script has its own rules. Our DTP operators are trained in the typographic conventions of every language we typeset.

Right-to-Left Languages

Arabic · Hebrew · Persian (Farsi) · Urdu · Kurdish

  • Fully mirrored layouts — page order, columns, icons and callouts flipped
  • Correct ligature shaping, kashida justification and diacritic placement
  • Bidirectional (bidi) text handling: embedded Latin brand names, numbers and URLs inside RTL paragraphs
  • RTL-aware tables, charts and image direction

CJK — Chinese, Japanese, Korean

Simplified & Traditional Chinese · Japanese · Korean

  • Proper CJK font selection and fallback chains — no tofu boxes
  • Line-breaking rules (kinsoku shori) and character spacing respected
  • Vertical text and ruby/furigana annotation support
  • Locale-correct punctuation and full-width/half-width handling

South & Southeast Asian Scripts

Hindi (Devanagari) · Thai · Vietnamese · Tamil · Bengali · Khmer · Lao · Burmese

  • Combining marks, vowel signs and tone marks rendered correctly
  • Line breaking for scripts without spaces between words
  • Complex-script shaping verified on final rendered pages, not just in text boxes

European, Cyrillic & More

German · French · Spanish · Russian · Polish · Greek · Turkish · and 40+ more

  • 30–40% text expansion absorbed without breaking the design
  • Language-correct hyphenation and diacritic display
  • Long compound words (German, Finnish, Dutch) handled gracefully

Workflow

Our DTP process

A repeatable five-step process that delivers consistent, print-ready files every time.

1
Source File Analysis
We analyse your files for font availability, text expansion/contraction ratios, embedded graphics, and any language-specific issues such as RTL text direction or CJK character spacing before work begins.
2
Translation & Terminology
Content is extracted and translated by subject-matter linguists. We apply your existing glossaries and translation memories to ensure consistency with previous publications.
3
DTP Production
Translated text is flowed back into the native file. Our DTP operators resolve text overflow, adjust font sizes, reflow callouts and captions, and adapt graphics containing embedded text.
4
DTP Quality Review
A separate DTP QA specialist checks every page for overflow, orphaned lines, incorrect hyphenation, font substitution errors, colour profile consistency, and visual alignment with the source layout.
5
Delivery & Preflight
Files are packaged with all linked assets and fonts. Print-ready PDFs pass a standard preflight check. We deliver native source files alongside PDFs so you can make future edits independently.
