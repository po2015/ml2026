A training program only works if every employee actually completes it — and completion collapses when the course reads like a translation. Multinationals rolling out compliance, safety, and product training globally face the same pattern: the English original performs, the localized versions underperform, and regional L&D teams start rebuilding from scratch.

The fix is localizing the whole learner experience, not just the slide text. Narration needs native-speaker delivery — human or well-engineered AI voices — timed to the original. Quizzes and feedback need cultural review. Embedded videos need subtitles or dubbing. And the rebuilt course must still report completion to your LMS exactly as the original does.

We localize courses end-to-end in the authoring tool you already use — Storyline, Rise, Captivate, Lectora — and validate every package against SCORM and xAPI before delivery, so your completion dashboards stay comparable across regions.
