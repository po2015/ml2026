Most industrial manufacturers reach global buyers through distributors and trade fairs — channels where the buyer relationship belongs to someone else. A manufacturer with its own multilingual website, localized catalogs, and native-language product videos changes that equation: buyers find you on Google, evaluate your specs in their language, and send the RFQ directly to you.

The bar is higher than it looks. Industrial buyers read spec sheets line by line, in German and Japanese as carefully as in English. A machine-translated tolerance table or a garbled model number does not just look unprofessional — it disqualifies you from the shortlist. The manufacturers winning direct international business treat multilingual content as production infrastructure, not marketing decoration.

We build and localize the full stack for manufacturers: fast multilingual websites with inquiry-optimized structure, product catalogs and technical manuals typeset in InDesign and FrameMaker, and product and training videos dubbed or subtitled for every market you sell into.
