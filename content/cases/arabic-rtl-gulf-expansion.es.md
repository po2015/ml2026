Arabic market entry fails quietly when it is treated as translation plus a CSS direction flip. Gulf buyers read machine-translated Arabic the way you read a badly translated manual — it signals that their market is an afterthought. Native copy, properly engineered RTL layout, and typesetting that respects the Arabic script tell the opposite story: that you built something for this market on purpose. Distributors notice the difference before the first meeting.
